Same idea, many products.

Everything we build stands on four pillars, whatever it does. Pick one to see how we work.

Build to usability

Our main priority is a usable product first. Everything else comes after.

Test and polish

Once it is usable, we test it and fix the mistakes and bugs.

Collect feedback

We watch how people use it and ask them what they need.

Improve

We use that feedback to make the product better, then the loop starts again.
